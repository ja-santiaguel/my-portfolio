## Decided
- **Feel:** brutalist (architectural: honest structure, mass over thin lines), confident and precise; fluid, fast and reactive; cool monochrome so the work stands out.
- **Story:** architecture shapes the structure. The kitchen is a brief mention only: constant testing and adjusting, and discipline.
- **Audience:** recruiters skim in 30–60 seconds; hiring managers read a case study in depth. Every page has to work both ways.
- **Inspiration:** [noth.in](https://www.noth.in/), for its range of effects and transitions, big bold type, scroll effects, and visuals that speak for themselves.

## Learned
A layout or interaction brief is not permission to change copy or type. When I asked for layout work and got rewritten copy and fonts, I had it all reverted. They're separate decisions, and I make them.
