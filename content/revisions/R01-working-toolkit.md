## What changed
Written standards my AI-assisted workflow applies every time (`.claude/skills/`):
- `frontend-design` and `impeccable`: design direction and generic defaults to avoid.
- `case-study-writer`: case study structure and voice.
- `ship-check`: a pre-deploy gate (build, mobile, accessibility, links, image weight, metadata).
- `revision-log`: this log, major milestones only.

Also added a `CLAUDE.md` that records goals, priorities, decisions and working rules for every session.

## Learned
Writing a standard down forces me to define it. Checking my own site against a list of generic defaults showed some of my habits were defaults, not choices.
